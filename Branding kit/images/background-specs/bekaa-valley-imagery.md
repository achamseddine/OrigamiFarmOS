# Bekaa Valley Imagery Direction

Use realistic Bekaa Valley agricultural scenery:

- Wide valley farmland and open crop fields.
- Orchards, vineyards, vegetable rows, and irrigation lines.
- Anti-Lebanon / Mount Lebanon ranges in the background.
- Rural Lebanese farm buildings, not Italian villas.
- Dry summer tones mixed with cultivated green fields.
- Avoid Tuscan cypress-heavy landscapes, Alpine barns, and American ranch aesthetics.
