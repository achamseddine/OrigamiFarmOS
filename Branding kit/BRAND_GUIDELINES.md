# Origami FarmOS Brand Guidelines — Option C Demo

## Brand feeling
Premium Lebanese agri-tech. Practical, warm, intelligent, and calm. Avoid generic ERP visuals, cartoon farming, and overly rustic styles.

## Primary identity
- Deep cedar green for trust, agriculture, and Lebanese identity.
- Warm stone/off-white backgrounds for calm tablet usability.
- Wheat gold for morning actions, harvest, and premium accents.
- Red only for real alerts.

## UI principles
- Morning-first: the manager starts with what needs attention.
- Operational, not promotional: app screens should avoid slogans that repeat daily.
- Evidence-first: alerts and AI recommendations explain why.
- Tablet-first: large touch targets, fast scanning, minimal typing.
- Lebanon-ready: Arabic/English, RTL support, mixed farm reality, weak connectivity.

## Logo usage
Use the full logo for landing/welcome and app loading screens. Use the mark only for nav rail, favicon, app icon, and compact badges.

## Imagery
Use authentic Lebanese agricultural landscapes, especially Bekaa Valley farmland, orchard rows, dry mountains, and local farm structures. Avoid imagery that feels Tuscan, Alpine, American ranch, or generic stock farming.
