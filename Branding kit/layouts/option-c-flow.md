# Option C Manager Demo Flow

1. **Welcome / Start My Day** — Manager starts the morning and enters the operational briefing.
2. **Morning Briefing Dashboard** — Priorities, alerts, weather, timeline, and today’s tasks.
3. **Animal Status Overview** — Herd/flock health state and animals needing attention.
4. **Animal Digital Twin** — One animal’s complete operational history and recommendations.
5. **Feed & Inventory** — Feed stocks, consumption, low-stock alerts, and feeding plan.
6. **Milk Production** — Milk totals, sessions, withdrawal warning, quality, and destination.
7. **Egg Production** — Flock-level egg output, allocation, and productivity insight.
8. **Health Intelligence** — Explainable recommendations from observations and history.
9. **Produce & Harvest** — Fields, harvest calendar, yield, inventory, ready-for-sale items.
10. **Sales, Expenses & Daily Summary** — Daily revenue, costs, gross margin, cash, pending payments, insights.
